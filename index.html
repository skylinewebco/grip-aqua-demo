Our story

We wanted a soda we'd be proud to put on the table.

SK began with a simple frustration: the drinks aisle was full of sugar bombs on one side and flavorless sparkling water on the other. Nothing in between felt considered.

So we built it. A soda with real fruit, plant fiber and a fraction of the sugar — wrapped in a can designed with the same care as what's inside. SK is taste, design and experience, refusing to compromise on any of them.
